Around Urakawa / Hidaka (Week 1)

🐴 Thoroughbred farms

Hidaka breeds most of Japan's racehorses. You can see horses grazing beside the roads, and some farms welcome visitors (keep behind the fences).

🏇 Urakawa riding experiences

Pony and horse rides for kids in Urakawa. Book ahead.

🦭 Cape Erimo

About 1 hour east of Urakawa. Dramatic windswept cape, with wild seals often seen on the rocks. Very windy, so bring jackets.

🌳 Nijukken Road, Shinhidaka

A long avenue of trees, famous for cherry blossoms in spring and pleasant in autumn colours.

🪶 Nibutani Ainu Culture Museum

In Biratori. Learn about Hokkaido's Indigenous Ainu culture. A good stop on the drive between Chitose and Urakawa.
Around Asahikawa / Higashikawa / Biei

🍜 Asahikawa Ramen Village

Eight famous ramen shops under one roof. Easy, warm lunch with kids.

🌾 Biei Patchwork Road

Rolling hills and iconic trees (Ken & Mary Tree, Seven Stars Tree). Fits on the drive to Furano on 14 Oct.

💦 Shirogane Shirahige Falls

Five minutes from the Blue Pond. A blue river and a waterfall seen from a bridge.

🚠 Asahidake Ropeway

About 40 minutes from Higashikawa. Early October is the tail end of the autumn colours, and there may be early snow at the top. Pack the down jackets.

🍶 Otokoyama Sake Brewery

Free museum and tasting in Asahikawa, for the grown-ups.

☕ Higashikawa town

Cute "photo town" with cafés and woodcraft shops. It's also known for having delicious tap water from the Taisetsu mountains.
Around Furano

🛍 Furano Marché

Local produce, snacks and souvenirs. Easy parking and toilets.

🍇 Furano Winery & Jam Garden

Free winery tour (grape juice for kids). The Jam Garden lets you try lots of jams.

🍦 Furano Delice / Cheese Factory ice cream

Soft serve and desserts made with local milk.

Useful info

🌡 Weather

About 5–15 °C, cold mornings and nights. Sunset is around 5 pm, so plan to finish outdoor activities by about 4:30 pm.

🚗 Driving

Bring your Singapore licence and an International Driving Permit. Japan also drives on the left. Child seats are required by law for kids under 6. Watch for deer on Hidaka roads at dusk. Fuel is "regular" (レギュラー). Ask the rental company for an ETC card for toll roads.

🐻 Bears

Autumn is a busy time for bears. Stick to marked paths, don't leave food out, and consider bear bells on hikes.

🛂 Arrival

Fill in Visit Japan Web before you fly and screenshot the QR codes for immigration and customs.

🔌 Power

Japan uses 100 V Type A (two flat pins). Singapore plugs need an adapter.

💴 Money

Cards are widely accepted, but small rural shops and cafés may be cash only. 7-Eleven ATMs take foreign cards. No tipping.

🛍 Tax-free

Spend ¥5,000 or more in one store and show your passport.

♨️ Onsen

Wash before entering, no swimwear, and tattoos may not be allowed.

📱 Connectivity

Get a Japan eSIM or roaming plan before you go. Download offline Google Maps for Hidaka, Asahikawa and Furano, because rural signal can be patchy.

🗑 Rubbish

Sort it at Airbnbs: burnable, plastic, cans/bottles. Check the house rules.

🚨 Emergency

Police 110 · Ambulance/Fire 119 · Japan Visitor Hotline 050-3816-2787 (24 h, English)
